About The Project

The Master Bookland ERP is a full production-ready Inventory Management System (built with the help of Lovable AI) actively utilized in a real-world business today. It empowers the bookstore client to seamlessly handle their vast daily inventory operations.

Featuring a robust secure authentication gate, the application routes authorized administrators to a deeply analytical dashboard. Features include real-time tracking of units in stock (such as tracking 38,000+ units, categorized by textbooks, novels, publishers, etc.), low stock alerts, dynamic purchase recording, return tracking, and overall automated ledger accounting.
